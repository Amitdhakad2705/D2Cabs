Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

At D2Cabs, we believe booking a cab should feel simple and trustworthy. When you use our website or services, you may share some personal information with us. This Privacy Policy explains in simple language what information we may collect, why we need it, how we use it, and the choices you have. We only use information for genuine business and service needs and aim to handle it responsibly.

For more information about India’s data-protection framework, you can read the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information We Collect

  • Basic details: When you contact us, create an account, or make a booking, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address and other details you choose to provide.
  • Booking details: We may collect your pickup point, drop location, travel date, preferred time, trip type, booking history and other information needed to arrange your journey.
  • Location information: If location access is enabled, we may use your location to help with pickup, navigation, ride coordination and other location-based features.
  • Payment information: If you pay online, payment details are handled through the relevant payment service provider. We may receive transaction information needed to confirm and manage your payment.
  • Device and usage information: Our website or app may collect basic technical information such as device type, browser, IP address, pages visited and information about how the service is used.
  • Communication information: If you contact our support team, we may keep the messages, call details or other information you provide so that we can understand and resolve your request.

How We Use Your Information

  • We use your information to confirm and manage bookings, arrange your cab and help make sure your trip runs smoothly.
  • We may use your contact details to send booking confirmations, driver or trip updates, important service messages and support responses.
  • Location information may be used to coordinate pickup and drop-off, provide navigation-related features and improve the accuracy of the service.
  • We may use booking and service information to understand what is working well and improve our website, booking experience, customer support and overall service.
  • Where permitted, we may use your details to share relevant offers or updates from D2Cabs. You can choose not to receive promotional communications where an opt-out option is available.

How We Share Information

  • To complete your booking, we may share necessary trip information with the driver or service partner assigned to your journey, such as your name, pickup point, drop location and contact information needed for coordination.
  • We may use trusted service providers for payments, technology, hosting, customer support, communications and other activities that help us operate D2Cabs. They receive only the information reasonably needed for their work.
  • We may share information when required by law, a valid legal request, or to protect the safety, rights and property of D2Cabs, our customers, drivers or other people.
  • We do not sell your personal information simply as a product to third parties.

Location and Ride Information

  • Cab services naturally depend on location information. We may use pickup and drop details to match your booking with the right driver and help complete the journey.
  • Some location features may continue to depend on the permissions you give through your device. You can manage location permissions from your phone settings, although some features may not work properly without them.
  • After a trip, information about the journey may be retained for legitimate purposes such as booking records, customer support, safety, dispute handling and legal or accounting requirements.

Payments and Transactions

  • For online payments, your payment may be processed by a third-party payment provider. D2Cabs does not need to keep your complete card or banking credentials simply to confirm a booking.
  • We may retain transaction references, payment status, booking amount and related records so that we can provide receipts, resolve payment issues and maintain accurate business records.

Cookies and Website Technology

  • Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand website usage, improve performance and make the experience more useful.
  • Some cookies may be provided by services we use for analytics, security or website functionality. You can control many cookie settings through your browser.

Customer Support and Feedback

  • When you contact D2Cabs for help, we may use the information you provide to investigate your issue, respond to you and improve our service.
  • If you share feedback, reviews, suggestions or complaints, we may use that information to improve our services and customer experience.

Keeping Your Information Safe

  • We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the information we handle against unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure.
  • Even with appropriate safeguards, no website, app or online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We continuously aim to improve the way we protect information.

How Long We Keep Information

  • We keep information for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide our services, maintain booking and transaction records, handle support requests, meet legal obligations and protect our legitimate interests.
  • When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we aim to delete it or keep it in a form that no longer directly identifies you, where appropriate.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can contact us to ask about the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.
  • If your information is incorrect or outdated, you can ask us to update or correct it.
  • You can ask us about deletion of your information where deletion is legally and operationally possible. Some records may need to be retained for legal, safety, accounting or transaction purposes.
  • You can manage certain device permissions, such as location access, through your phone or browser settings.
  • You may choose not to receive promotional communications where an unsubscribe or opt-out option is provided. Service-related messages may still be sent when they are necessary for your booking or account.

Children's Privacy

  • D2Cabs services are intended for users who can legally use the service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for independent use of our services where such collection is not permitted.
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the situation.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • As D2Cabs grows, our services, technology and legal requirements may change. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect those changes.
  • When we make an important change, we will take reasonable steps to make the updated policy noticeable on our website. We encourage you to check this page occasionally so you know how your information is being handled.

Contact D2Cabs

  • If you have a question, concern or request about this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your information, please contact the D2Cabs team at d2cabs@gmail.com or +91 88896 33535.
  • We will try to understand your concern and respond as reasonably and clearly as possible.
